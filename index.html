About Us

Empowering young minds through purposeful learning.

Butemba Modern Nursery and Primary School is committed to developing learners who are confident, disciplined, and ready to succeed in life.

We provide a balanced education that combines academic growth with religious and moral teaching, ensuring that children grow into responsible individuals who contribute positively to their families and communities.

✓ Safe learning environment Children are supported in a warm and nurturing setting.
